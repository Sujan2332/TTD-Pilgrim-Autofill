Note:
1.
A pilgrim can avail the darshan of Sri Padmavathi Ammavaru, Tiruchanoor by booking 'Special Entry Darshan (Sri PAT)' tickets well-in advance through ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in. Please read the Instructions before proceeding with booking.
2.
Pilgrims without valid Darshan tickets will not be allowed to SRI PAT, Tiruchanoor
3.
At the time of entry, all pilgrims shall produce the same original Photo ID used during booking. Age proof shall be produced for children below 12 years to gain entry free of cost.
4.
All bookings are FINAL: Postponement/advancement/cancellation/refund is not allowed.
